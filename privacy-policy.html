Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Climb or Fall ("the Game"), developed by Skyforge Engines, is designed to respect and protect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and the choices you have regarding this data. By downloading and playing the Game, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Data: We do not collect, request, or require any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number) to play the Game. The Game is fully playable without registering a user account.

Automatically Collected Data: To help us optimize the game, troubleshoot bugs, and provide in-game advertisements, third-party services integrated within the Game (specifically Unity Ads) may automatically collect certain technical and usage statistics, which may include:

  • Your IP address and network connection details.
  • Device technical specifications, including manufacturer, hardware model, operating system version, and system language.
  • Mobile advertising identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID or Apple ID for Advertisers - IDFA).
  • Game performance data, error reports, and crash logs.

3. Local Data Storage

Your game progress, including your high scores, accumulated coins, and unlocked skins, is saved locally on your device using Web Storage (LocalStorage) or IndexedDB. This data remains on your physical device and is not uploaded to our servers. Please note that clearing your app storage, clearing browser cache (if running on web), or uninstalling the Game will permanently erase your local game progress, and we cannot recover it for you.

4. Third-Party Services and Advertising

We integrate third-party advertising SDKs to show advertisements and monetize the Game. These providers collect information under their own privacy policies. Our primary service provider is:

Unity Ads: Used to deliver interstitial and rewarded video ads. Unity Ads collects device information and advertising identifiers to target relevant ads. You can read Unity's Privacy Policy, cookie policies, and data handling procedures at Unity's Player Privacy Policy.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

We are committed to protecting children's privacy. Climb or Fall is a general audience mobile game. We do not knowingly collect or request personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). The integrated Unity Ads SDK is configured to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) regarding children's data. If we discover that personal data has been inadvertently collected from a child under the required age, we will take immediate steps to delete that data.

6. Your Rights & Choice (GDPR and CCPA)

Depending on your location, you may have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to access, delete, or restrict processing of your technical data.

Opting Out of Personalized Ads: You can choose to restrict or opt-out of interest-based advertising by adjusting the privacy settings of your mobile device:

  • Android: Open your Settings app, go to Privacy or Google > Ads, and select "Opt out of Ads Personalization" or "Reset Advertising ID".
  • iOS: Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track" or toggle tracking permission off specifically for the Game.

7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard safeguards to secure the technical data collected by our third-party services. However, because your gameplay data (coins, skins, progress) is stored locally on your device, its security also depends on how you secure your mobile device.
